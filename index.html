RestroPilot: item-demand forecasting

RestroPilot is a team capstone for restaurant operations. I own the machine-learning part: a Random Forest that forecasts each menu item's daily recorded quantity from historical takeaway orders.

  • Cleaned and reconciled 194,001 order lines and 550 catalogue items from two restaurants, keeping source provenance and flagging ambiguous duplicates rather than dropping them.
  • Engineered 1/7/14/28-day lag and 7/28-day rolling-mean features using only past data. Training, validation, and test periods are split chronologically.
  • Cut test MAE from 5.569 to 4.172 units per scored item-day (about 25%) against a same-item previous-week baseline. Results are reported per item, with coverage of excluded days.
  • Packages the model as a versioned artifact with SHA-256 verification. The service refuses to load a missing or modified model.
  • Python
  • pandas
  • NumPy
  • scikit-learn
  • joblib

Research model trained on 2017–2019 data. It predicts recorded quantities, not verified demand, and is not deployed to production. The team repository isn't public.
